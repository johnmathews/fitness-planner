One week, Monday to Sunday, as a grid: a row per exercise or food, and a DayMark per day.

## Use

- **Filling the screen (K24):** the grid takes the height its PlannerScreen leaves it, and its sizes follow the screen through one scale, `u`: 1 on a 1280×800 screen, `min(100vw / 1280, 100dvh / 800)`, held between 0.75 and 1.6. The name column is 280u and the seven days share the rest; there is no Week column (K33). Rows share the height equally, at least 56px each. They stop at 1.25 times a day's width, so a tall screen centres the week rather than stretching it. With more rows than fit, each keeps its 56px and the screen runs longer. Each day cell is a size container, and its DayMark fills 86% of the cell's shorter side, up to 128px (DayMark, Filling a cell). The type grows with `u` and never drops below the system's 16px: names max(18px, 24u), chips max(16px, 17u), day names max(16px, 20u). At 1280×800 the boxes are 86px.
- **Short screens (540px tall or less, a phone on its side):** the SectionHeader's line goes into the grid's empty top-left cell (the title), so five rows still fit at 56px. The chips and the PlannerScreen's way on drop out.
- **Folded (held upright under 1000px wide, or any screen under 760px wide; K26):** the same grid, folded so nothing scrolls sideways. Each row's name and chips share one line across the seven days, and its seven boxes sit beneath. The name column is gone. Sizes follow a second scale, `n`: `100vw / 600`, held between 1 and 1.4, so a phone keeps the system's sizes and an iPad held upright grows them. A row of boxes is `clamp(48px, min(19vw, 7.5dvh), 110px)` tall.
- **Header:** the day in `label` ("Mon", sentence case, never "MON"), and nothing else: no date (K29). The date stays visually hidden for a screen reader, and each DayMark's name says the full date. A 1px `ink` rule closes the header.
- **Rows:** the name in `body` at weight 600, then the Chip(s) for sets × reps and weight. The goal doesn't show on the grid (K33): only the item's dialog says it. Rows are separated by 1px `line` hairlines; no vertical rules, no boxed cells, no card around the grid.
- **Today:** no band (K36): today looks like every other day. Its header says ", today" and its marks "today" for a screen reader, and today's first mark is the grid's tab stop.
- **No Week column** (K33): the grid has no row totals ("3 of 4"). A week's progress shows in its marks and in the days that turn green.
- **No Picked row** (K28): the grid has no row counting each day against the daily pick.
- **A day that meets the daily pick** (exercises only; K30, K39): once a day's marked exercises reach the pick ("3 a day"), its name turns `pen` with a 16px Lucide `check` after it, and a green marker stroke underlines its column, just below the last row: filled `pen`, 88% of the column wide, max(18px, 24u) tall (12px folded), bowed, tapered to rounded ends and uneven at the edges, as if drawn with a whiteboard marker. There are three strokes (DayMark's `MET_UNDERLINES`) and the days take them in turn, so neighbours differ. A visually hidden ", daily goal met" follows the day's name in its header. The stroke prints too.
- **Food:** the same grid without chips. A food's day has one kind: pressing toggles it between empty and great.
- **Edit plan:** each row's edit (`pencil`) IconButton follows its name, 4px after it, with a -10px vertical margin so the name line keeps its height and the button keeps its 44px target. It opens the item's dialog (Edit exercise, Edit food), where everything about it changes, the goal ("Times a week") included, so rows have no Stepper. The remove (`trash-2`) IconButton ends the row, in a 56px column after the days whose header is a visually hidden "Remove". The name column widens by the edit button and its gap (52px), the same in both grids so their days line up. The name block is at least 44px tall, an IconButton's target, so rows keep their height when Edit plan toggles (F25). Folded, the edit button follows the name on its line and the remove button ends the line.
- **Print:** the light theme on white paper, from either theme (K16). Words, boxes and marks keep the light theme's colours; the surfaces go transparent, chips are outlined. A section hint, when there is one, and every button stay off paper (F24). On paper the grid is a table again, at the fixed sizes: a 64px DayMark in a 76px cell, and a 200px name column. A table split across pages repeats its `<thead>`. In the app, put the token overrides shown in the preview's `.print` rule under `@media print`; they are the only literal colours the system allows.

## Accessibility

- A `<table role="grid">` (the WAI-ARIA grid pattern), laid out on screen as a CSS grid: the table is `display: grid` and its row groups and rows are `display: contents`, so the cells place themselves and the explicit roles keep the table's meaning with an `aria-label` naming the section and week ("Exercises, week of 19 October"). Every `<tr>` is `role="row"`. Day headers are `<th scope="col" role="columnheader">`; today's has `aria-current="date"` and a visually hidden ", today". Row names are `<th scope="row" role="rowheader">`, so a screen reader reads "Push-ups" with each cell. Day cells are `<td role="gridcell">`. 
- **One tab stop per grid** (roving tabindex): today's cell in the first row is the single tab stop (`tabindex="0"`); every other cell is `tabindex="-1"`. Tab lands there (or on the last cell used); Tab again leaves the grid. Every cell joins the navigation: the row names, the day cells and Edit plan's remove cells. Arrow keys move cell by cell across all of them; Home and End go to the first and last cell in the row; Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End to the first and last cell of the grid. Only the day cells act on Space and Enter: they step the day's mark (an exercise empty → great → partial → missed → empty; a food on and off). On any other cell those keys do nothing. Focus never wraps to another row. In Edit plan a cell's controls (the edit button in the name cell, the remove button in the last cell) are kept out of the Tab order; Enter or F2 on the cell lets them in, Tab moves among them, and Escape puts focus back on the cell. In a day cell the focus lands on its DayMark button (the cell's one widget); other cells take focus themselves. This brings the planner from about 77 tab stops to two (F15).
- Each DayMark is a `<button aria-pressed>` named "Push-ups, Thursday 22 October, today" ("…, partly done" or "…, couldn't do it" for those kinds); see DayMark for the full pattern.
- The focus ring is a solid 2px `focus` outline at 2px offset, never clipped: the mark fills 86% of its cell, so the ring stays inside it. Cells without a mark (names) draw the same ring inset by 2px.

## You provide

- The week's days, with today's index (or none, for another week).
- Rows: name, chips (from the exercise's sets, reps or duration, per side and weight), weekly goal, seven marks (`null | 'great' | 'partial' | 'missed'`) and which days have a note.
- For exercises, which days meet the daily pick.
- The press handler, which steps the day with `nextMark` (`EXERCISE_CYCLE` or `FOOD_CYCLE`), and the `aria-label` for the grid.
