A Settings page like the week

Settings is a narrow 576px column at the system's small fixed sizes, under a bar and above a week that are now full width and big. Both options take the week's language: the page's own 48u margins, everything sized from the screen through u, the title in the serif at 40u like “Exercises”, headings at 27u like the row names, labels and text at 19u, controls 60u tall, and marks 60u. They differ in how the page uses the width.

  • Same contents, order and copy as now; nothing new to set.
  • The controls keep their cards' shapes and colours, drawn at the page's size; nothing new to measure for contrast.
  • On a phone both fold to one column at the system's sizes, as now.

Round 2 (decided: A). From your notes: no bar on the Settings page, so everything moves up, and the data and account buttons sit beside their text, as the counter does.
