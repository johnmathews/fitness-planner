4. The snap (decided: the new snap)

Today the page waits until a scroll has stopped, then jumps. With the new snap, the jump starts as soon as the next screen’s edge comes into view by a step (4% of the window), while your fingers or the trackpad’s momentum are still moving. It takes a quarter of a second and ignores the rest of that flick. On a phone, the jump starts when your finger lifts. A screen with more rows than fit still scrolls normally until its end.
