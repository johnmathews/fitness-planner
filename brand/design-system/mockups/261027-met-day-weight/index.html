A day whose marks reach Exercises a day turns green. Decided in round 1: B. The stroke under the column goes, and one pen line, max(2px, 3u), runs under the day's name and its check together. The check is bigger and heavier: 1.15 times the name, with a 2.5 stroke instead of 1.5.

The question now: how big are the day names? They are 20u today. Both options apply to every day, met or not, and to Food's days too. On a phone, a column is too narrow for a name and its check, so the check stays off there: the green underlined name shows it alone.
