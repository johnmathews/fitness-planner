Bigger row names

Each line of a row's name cell goes up one size: the exercise name, the sets-and-reps chip and the note. The question is whether the name column widens to make room. The boxes, the marks, the day names and the title stay as they are.

One step on the system's type scale, on a phone and at the smallest sizes:

  • Name: 18 to 20 (body to body-large), weight 600
  • Chip: 16 to 18, weight 600
  • Note: 16 to 18, serif italic

On a larger screen the type grows with it, by the same step: at 1280×800 the name goes from 24 to 27 and the chip and note from 17 to 19. No colour changes, so contrast stays as it is.

Try it full size, on your own screen or phone. The switch at the bottom left changes the size and the theme.
