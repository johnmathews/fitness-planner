A full-screen week

The planner becomes one screen with one job, like the timer: the week fills the screen, and the bar stays out of sight until it is wanted. Scrolling down brings Food, which takes the whole screen in turn. Two questions: how the bar is reached and where each screen’s title sits (A, B or C), and what a phone held upright shows (P1 or P2).

Every option shares these:

  • Exercises is one screen and Food is the next. The scroll stops on each, never between.
  • The grid grows with the screen. On a 1280×800 desktop the names are 24px and the boxes 86px. Nothing drops below the system’s 16px.
  • An iPad, and a phone on its side, get the same screen as a desktop.
  • On a tall screen the week stays a sensible shape and sits in the middle. With more rows than fit, rows stop at 56px and the screen runs longer.
  • The marks, pens and the bar’s contents stay as they are. The marks here are your Stamped ticks.
  • Round 2, from your comments: no Picked row, and the day headers show only the day’s name. Round 3: no section figures (“15 of 15 this week”, “0 of 4 goals met”). Round 4: no Week column, and a Full screen button.

The screens on this page are live. Hover the top edge to bring the bar, scroll to reach Food, press a box to mark it. Open the full-size version on a desktop, an iPad or a phone to try it at its real size. Its Mockup button switches option and theme.
