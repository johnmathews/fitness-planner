A bigger bar

The week is bold and big; the bar over it is small and thin. The bar stays out of sight until the top edge, the handle or the keyboard brings it (K25), so it can be as big as the page. Every option here spans the full width, on the page's own margins: the wordmark sits over “Exercises” and the last action over Sunday. The week, its arrows and the wordmark grow too. The rows below the bar stay as built.

  • The bar scales with the screen like the week does: about 108px tall at 1280×800, and 163px on a screen your size (1920×1200), against 56px now.
  • Icons stay Lucide outlines at the system's 1.5 stroke; at 32px and up that draws a 2px line or more, so they read as solid.
  • In Edit plan and on Settings the bar stays in view, as now (K27), so it takes that much of the page there.
  • No new colours: Edit plan pressed uses the selected segment's ink fill, never the pen.

Round 3: question 3, where Edit plan sits, is open, at the top. Questions 1 and 2 are settled; their frames show round 2, with Full screen last at the far right and Edit plan beside the wordmark.
