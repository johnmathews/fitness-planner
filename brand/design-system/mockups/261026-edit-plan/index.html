Edit plan like the week

Edit plan is still a 1152px column at the system's small sizes. Both options give it the week's language, as the Settings page now has:

  • Full width on the page's 48u margins, every size from u.
  • Each section titled like its screen: “Exercises” in the 40u serif with the week beside it. The page's own heading stays for a screen reader, as on the planner.
  • Rows at the week's sizes (names 27u, chips and notes 19u, 20u day names) and a fixed 100u tall, since the page scrolls rather than filling the window.
  • Edit and remove at 52u with 28u icons, and “Copy plan to next week” as a 60u button on the Exercises title line.
  • On a phone the week folds as on the planner, with edit and remove on the name's line.

The question is how a new exercise or food is added. Decided: B, a button that opens a dialog (K54).
