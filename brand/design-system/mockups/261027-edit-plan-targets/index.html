Daily targets in Edit plan

Edit plan has a daily target on each section's title line (K56, K57). Round 3 centred it under the bar's week at max(44px, 40u), its words beside it (K62), and that is what is built.

Round 4 tries the words two other ways, at the same place and size: stacked over the Stepper, or gone, with the Stepper's value reading “4 per day”. A screen reader still hears “Exercises a day” in both. The Steppers work.
